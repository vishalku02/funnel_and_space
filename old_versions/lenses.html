A short situation

Meet Alex.

Alex is six weeks into learning to code. For the last hour, they've been stuck on the same exercise — moving a small robot through a grid using a loop. Their code keeps timing out. They've tried eight things. None worked.

The course is built around a community: short lecture videos, weekly section meetings with a leader, a discussion forum where other learners post about the exact same exercises. Alex hasn't opened the forum yet.

Alex is at home. It's Sunday evening. They could be doing other things.
